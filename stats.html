Our Little World

How much it gets played — in numbers that belong to nobody.

reading…

What is counted. Worlds, spots taken, minutes played, the day a world reached, what got built, what got done. A spot is one role in one world on one day — as close to “a person” as this gets. Two spots is a parent and a child, or one person playing twice; nothing here can tell those apart.

What is never counted. No addresses, no device ids, no world names, no times of day. A calendar day is the finest grain kept anywhere, and a world that is forgotten leaves its numbers behind without its name.
